Quick help
How do I withdraw money?

Open Wallet, tap Withdraw, choose UPI, Paytm, Gift Card or Mobile Recharge, fill in the details and submit the request. A 3% TDS & GST deduction applies only on UPI and Paytm withdrawals (for example, on a ₹100 UPI/Paytm withdrawal you receive ₹97; Gift Card and Mobile Recharge have no TDS/GST deduction).

How long does a withdrawal take?

UPI, Paytm and Gift Card requests target 3 hours. Mobile Recharge targets 30 minutes. These are targets, not guarantees.

How does Spin & Earn work?

You get 1 free spin every 24 hours and win 1 to 10 points. 10 points = ₹1.00 (1 point = ₹0.10), and you can convert 10 to 5,000 points at a time.

My ad reward was not added

Watch the full ad and tap Claim at the end. If the reward is still missing, message us on WhatsApp with a screenshot.
