1. Open https://search.google.com/search-console/ and add the exact URL-prefix property:
   https://playearn-arena-download.onrender.com/
2. Choose HTML tag verification and copy the complete `google-site-verification` token.
3. Add the meta tag to the homepage <head>, deploy, then click Verify in Search Console.
4. Submit https://playearn-arena-download.onrender.com/sitemap.xml.
5. Inspect the homepage URL and request indexing after the live deployment is confirmed.

Do not publish a guessed verification token. Search Console verification is not complete until Google confirms ownership.

## Backlink strategy (manual, quality-first)
- Publish a real PlayEarn Arena launch/update post on the official social profiles and link to the download page.
- Ask relevant Android gaming communities and app-discovery sites for an editorial review; disclose that the current APK is a beta preview.
- Submit only to legitimate app directories that accept Android beta apps and have editorial review.
- Contact gaming creators with a review APK and a clear disclosure; never pay for undisclosed endorsements.
- Ask relevant partners/community sites for a contextual link only where the app is genuinely relevant.
- Track each placement, live URL, anchor text, date, and referral traffic in a backlink register.
- Avoid link farms, automated comments, paid bulk backlinks, keyword-stuffed anchors, and reciprocal-link schemes.
